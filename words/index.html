    How to play

    • Make words across or down. The first word covers the centre star; later words join what's on the board.
    • 3W 2W triple or double the word. 3L 2L triple or double the letter.
    • Using all 7 tiles in one go earns 50 bonus points. Blank tiles can be any letter and score 0.
    • The game ends when the bag is empty and someone uses their last tile, or after six scoreless turns in a row. Tiles left on a rack count against you.
